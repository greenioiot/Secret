ZECRET — FEATURE SUMMARY



1. MEETINGS AND TRANSCRIPTION
- Record meetings with live captions and retained local audio.
- Thai/English transcription with configurable caption engines and
device selection.
- Optional Whisper accurate pass and guarded AI context corrections.
- End meeting to process, or cancel to stop without starting processing.
- Meeting list, search, sorting, transcript playback, speaker
identification,
   participation statistics, AI notes and Markdown export.
- Optional display of CPU, NPU, GPU, RAM and disk storage information.
- Responsive pages and wider meeting, voice and vocabulary tables.

2. TRANSCRIPT REVIEW AND FEEDBACK
- Review & improve section inside each meeting.
- Replay audio, correct text and retain original wording plus correction
history.
- Undo the latest text change; reject stale edits from another tab.
- Reviewed lines are protected from accurate/context passes; full
retranscription
   is blocked when it would erase review history.
- Regenerate notes explicitly from the corrected transcript.
- Suggest short Thai/English terms and transliterations for a business
domain.
- Suggestions are disabled until an administrator reviews and enables them.
- Corrections are meeting-specific, not automatic global replacements.
- No automatic speech-model training or measured accuracy improvement is
claimed.

3. VOCABULARY WORKSPACE
- Separate management of business domains, context, keywords and aliases.
- Thai, English and transliteration fields, with search and filtering.
- Append/import vocabulary with UTF-8, file-size, format and entry
validation.
- Revision checks protect against stale dictionary edits.
- Insurance vocabulary, including law, acts, brokers and Thailand's OIC.
- Added 100 corporate-meeting terms and 100 business-services terms.
- Enabled entries guide transcription/context within model hint limits.
- Compact single-line table rows with horizontal scrolling where needed.
- Shared vocabulary management is restricted to administrators under RBAC.

4. VOICE LIBRARY AND MANUAL ENROLLMENT
- Separate Enroll voice and Voice library pages.
- Record or upload samples; preview before enrollment.
- Manage names, samples, notes and playback of retained samples.
- Add samples to an existing person; replacement requires explicit action.
- Quality checks for sufficient speech, consistency and multiple voices.
- Local voiceprints and up to 40 seconds of sample speech retained for
playback.
- Remembering a meeting speaker's voice is opt-in with participant consent.
- Shared voice-library management is administrator-controlled.

5. EXPERIMENTAL HANDS-FREE ENROLLMENT
- Optional Thai/English spoken guidance using an installed local speech
voice.
- Browser Start and microphone permission are required; not an always-on
service.
- Combined wake/command support, e.g. "Hey Zecret, enroll my voice".
- Thai command variations, polite endings, command retries and pause
detection.
- Microphone gain, prompt volume and wake-sensitivity controls.
- Sensitivity percentages represent presets, not calibrated recognition
accuracy.
- New flow: consent -> speak -> automatic quality stop -> ask name ->
confirm/save.
- Quality checks begin around 22 seconds and repeat about every 5 seconds.
- Target: at least 20 seconds of detected speech, voice consistency >= 0.75,
   and passing single-voice analysis. Capture is capped at 40 seconds.
- Quality consistency is NOT a guaranteed recognition-accuracy percentage.
- After passing, ask naturally in Thai: "ขอทราบชื่อของคุณด้วยครับ".
- Introduce yourself with "ผมชื่อ ..." or "ฉันชื่อ ..." and confirm the name.
- No Stop command is needed to finish sample capture.
- Failed checks, cancellation or unconfirmed names do not save a sample.
- Name/confirmation clips are excluded from the enrolled voice sample.
- On-screen status and spoken completion feedback; no camera/face/pose
detection.
- Capture/decoding gaps remain; real microphone performance needs user
testing.

6. AI SUMMARY EDITING
- Meeting owner can edit and save an existing summary.
- Server checks ownership and blocks stale saves.
- Author, timestamp, before and after text are retained in
summary-history.json.
- Regenerating AI notes may replace manually edited summary text.
- Notes instructions require evidence for owners/deadlines and
unspecified labels
   when unsupported. Generated notes still require human review.
- Email (optional, Settings → Bot, email & network): sends the summary and report
  through the administrator's SMTP account. Every account keeps its own To and CC
  (Settings → Accounts → My account); the "Email…" dialog on a meeting shows
  them, with a CC field and "remember for next time". Members can e-mail any
  meeting they can read; the automatic send goes to the owner's recipients.

7. ACCOUNTS AND ROLE-BASED ACCESS
- Local sign-in with Admin, Member and Viewer roles.
- All signed-in users can view meetings; there is no per-meeting
read-sharing ACL.
- Members/Admins can create meetings; new meetings belong to their creator.
- Only the owner, with a Member/Admin role, can edit, delete, resume
recording,
   process or save the meeting summary. Another Admin cannot override
ownership.
- Viewers are read-only, including for meetings they may formerly have
owned.
- Admin manages accounts, roles, enabled states and shared
settings/services.
- Existing meetings stay unowned/read-only until Admin assigns an
eligible owner.
- Assignment does not allow later reassignment of already-owned meetings.
- Server enforcement covers HTTP writes and recording WebSockets.
- Cookie sessions expire after eight hours; logout/permission changes
revoke them.
- Password hashing, login throttling and CSRF protection; no default
credentials.
- Passwords require at least 12 characters.
- Remote sign-in requires HTTPS; loopback HTTP is allowed on this computer.
- Bulk cross-meeting text reapply/voice rematch are blocked under
ownership rules.
- This is local RBAC, prepare for enterprise SSO (dummy page first).

8. SIGN-IN DESIGN
- Modern two-panel Zecret layout with responsive mobile presentation.
- Light/dark theme support and reduced navigation clutter before sign-in.
- Show/hide password, Caps Lock warning, loading state and clear error
messages.
- First-administrator setup guidance shown when no accounts exist.
- People talking at the same time: overlapping speech is detected (pyannote segmentation), left out when learning voices, and separated (Conv-TasNet) so each person gets their own line, marked ⇄ with the mixed text kept (Settings -> Participants, on by default). Overlapped-part error rate 47.7% -> 20.6% in tests.
- PERFORMANCE.md: models and measured speed/accuracy for every activity.
- No words lost when people talk at once: short remarks said over someone are added as their own line (⇄+), quieter second voices kept when
  they are clearly another person, words heard only in the mixed sound shown as "Also heard"; "⇄ Check these lines" filter. Live captions with
  two voices are marked ⇄ (host, phones, Live tab).
- Participants' own microphones: on the join page a participant taps 🎤; their phone's audio is kept with the meeting, captioned live under
  their name (the room's copy of the same words is hidden) and, after the meeting, aligned to the room recording so their lines come from their
  phone while other people's words said at the same time stay. Settings -> Join (on by default). Tests: overlapped part A 36.7% -> 9.6%,
  B 38.0% -> 15.8%.
- Best transcript after the meeting: three Thai models (Pathumma Whisper Large, Pathumma Whisper medium, Pathumma FastConformer) transcribe
  every line and the text two of them agree on is kept (5.25% errors vs 6.65% before; about 4-5 h for a 1-hour meeting; the summary follows).
  Lines where they disagree show the other models' versions: one click puts one in the line (Transcript and Review tabs, "Models disagree"
  filter). Recording without live captions is the default (Record only).
- Processing window: meetings are transcribed and summarized after working hours (default 18:00-06:00 and all weekend; an administrator sets
  it in Settings -> Transcription). If the window closes mid-way the work stops at a checkpoint and continues the next evening. An
  administrator can press "Process now" on any waiting meeting.
- Expected ready time for every meeting: the Meetings list shows a "Processing queue" card and each waiting meeting's badge says when it
  starts and when it will be ready (transcript, who said what, summary step by step), estimated from this box's past meetings.
- Participants can pass the meeting on: the join page has a "▦ QR" button that shows the meeting's QR code full screen, so the people next to
  them scan it from their phone and join too (with a Copy link button).
- Sound check before every meeting: the page asks you to say a short sentence, sends it through the real recording path and shows the
  level, the room noise, and the words the speech model heard, next to the server / worker / disk / memory / storage / model checks. A
  silent or muted microphone cannot start a meeting; a quiet or noisy one gets a warning with what to do.
- During the meeting the app watches the microphone: no sound for a minute -> a red warning under the timer (and in the meeting's history);
  an unplugged USB microphone is replaced by the default one at once and the recording goes on; the level meter also works in Record-only mode.
- Participants' phones get the same care: tapping 🎤 on the join page runs a sound check first (say a sentence; the phone shows the words
  heard, or why the microphone is not usable yet), the host sees ✓/!/✕ next to each phone, and a phone that goes silent during the meeting
  is warned and flagged to the host.
- Pressing Start asks how this meeting should run: Live captions (what appears where, which model) or Record only (lightest, transcript
  afterwards), with a recommendation when the box is already processing another meeting. Then the system check runs for that choice.

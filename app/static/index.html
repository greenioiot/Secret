Performance & comparison · สถิติและการเปรียบเทียบ

Every finished meeting keeps its numbers (speed per stage, CPU / RAM / temperature, which engine and model made it, live caption delay, confidence, how far the engines disagree, how many lines you corrected, your rating). Compare any metric across engines, models and modes to see which setup really performs better. Meetings made before this existed are filled in from their history. There is no hand-corrected reference text, so quality is shown through proxies: confidence, disagreement, your corrections and your rating.

Export CSV

How much each named participant spoke in the meetings of the chosen dates (same From / To as above). It measures speaking time, turns and questions, not the quality of what was said or whether someone listened. Suggestions from transcript review · คำที่ผู้ตรวจทานเสนอ

Terms proposed by reviewers (or found in their corrections). They do nothing until you approve one into a pack; corrections themselves only change their own meeting.

Vocabulary packs · ชุดคำศัพท์เฉพาะทาง

Domain vocabulary for Thai + English meetings (Banking, Insurance, Regulator, Law & Acts, or your own). Each entry is a term written the way you want to read it, plus the Thai spellings a recogniser may produce for it. Packs that are on fix live captions, transcripts and summaries, and give the “Best” speech mode its context list. Choose the packs per meeting on the Record tab or in a meeting’s Plan tab; packs switched on here are the default.

Voice library · คลังเสียง

Voiceprints let Zecrets put a name on a participant automatically. They are biometric data: stored only on this device, collected with the person's consent, deleted on request. For playback, up to 40 seconds of each person's own speech is kept. Only administrators can change the library or play samples.

Hands-free enrolment (experimental) · ลงทะเบียนเสียงแบบไม่ต้องใช้มือ

Press Start and allow the microphone (nothing listens before that, and it stops when you press Stop or the enrolment ends). Then say . The guide asks for consent, records until the sample is good (checks start after about 22 s, every ~5 s, at most 40 s: target 20 s of speech, consistency ≥ 0.75, one voice), asks the name (“ผมชื่อ … / ฉันชื่อ …”) and confirms it. You never need to say Stop. Name and confirmation answers are not part of the sample. A failed check, a cancel or an unconfirmed name saves nothing. Speech is recognised on this device; the guide speaks with the installed local voice.

Sensitivity percentages are matching presets, not a measured recognition accuracy. No camera, face or pose detection is used.

Who is this? · ใครพูด

Participants who were never named, longest talk first. Listen to a few seconds, then name them or say “Not them”. Suggestions come from the meeting’s participant list and, if switched on in Settings, from voices. A suggestion is never proof: in your meetings the same person scored as low as 0.29 and two different people as high as 0.67. Naming changes the meeting, so only its owner can do it.

Capacity & limits · ขีดจำกัดการใช้งาน

Zecrets keeps the recording and live captions smooth: people are refused when free RAM or CPU would drop below these limits. The real limit can be lower than the number you set.

Join from the local network · เข้าร่วมผ่านเครือข่าย (optional)

Allow participants to join a meeting from their own device Add people who join to the meeting's participants
Participants may send their own phone's microphone (they tap 🎤 on the join page; their words come out clean even when people talk at once)

Off by default. When on, you choose per meeting (Record tab) whether to open joining. Guests follow the live captions through a zecret link that works while the meeting is being recorded: they cannot open recordings, other meetings or settings. If you allow it, a participant can also tap 🎤 to send their own phone's microphone: it is kept with the meeting as their own track (the “mics” folder of the meeting) and used for their lines in the transcript. Only share the link with people in the room. Names are typed by the guests and are not verified. Phones show a certificate warning once (this box uses a self-signed certificate): tap “Advanced” and continue.

Speech recognition on the NPU · ถอดเสียงด้วย NPU

Run the Typhoon Thai recogniser on the Hexagon NPU Show the sentence while it is being spoken (interim captions, needs the NPU)

The live captions and the quick transcript use the NPU (about 10× faster, less CPU). If the NPU cannot be used the CPU takes over automatically. The accurate Whisper pass still runs on the CPU.

Processing order · ลำดับการประมวลผล

Run the accurate (Whisper) transcription while the meeting is still being recorded (slower CPU cores, low priority) so little is left to do after you stop Transcribe first, then work out who said what as a follow-up task (off = identify participants first)

Only used when accuracy is “accurate”. The transcript is produced by one worker lane and everything after it (participants, summary, report) by another.

Planned meetings & documents · แผนและเอกสาร

Use a meeting's agenda and uploaded documents Repair near-miss spellings of known names / terms in the transcript (the original text is kept) Also quote relevant passages of the documents to the summary model (experimental: measured no gain, small risk of mixing document facts into what was said)

Plan a meeting in the Meetings tab, add its agenda and documents, then start the recording from the plan. The system reads the documents on this device to find names and terms, repairs their spelling in the transcript, and gives the summary the agenda (it then reports per agenda item and says which items were never discussed).

Accounts & roles · บัญชีผู้ใช้

Admin: accounts, roles and shared settings (vocabulary, word collection, voice library, e-mail…). Member: records meetings and changes the meetings they own. Viewer: reads everything, changes nothing. Every signed-in person can read every meeting. Only a meeting's owner (Member/Admin) can edit, process, continue or delete it — another Admin cannot override that.
